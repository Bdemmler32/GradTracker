Graduation Pathways

Pennsylvania Act 158 of 2018 — 5 Pathways to Graduation

Pathway Eligibility: Not Yet Determined Complete at least one full pathway to meet graduation requirements.
Under Act 158 of 2018, Pennsylvania students must demonstrate readiness through at least one of five pathways. Satisfying any single pathway completely meets the graduation requirement.

Pathway Status

Overall Progress

Pathway 1 — Keystone Proficiency

Score Proficient or Advanced on all three Keystone Exams

Score Proficient or Advanced (minimum scaled score of 1500 or better) on each Keystone Exam: Algebra I, Literature, and Biology. All three must be Proficient+. Benchmarks are editable in Testing Benchmarks.

Pathway 2 — Keystone Composite

Composite score across Keystone Exams meeting minimum thresholds

3-Score Composite: At least one score Proficient+, none Below Basic, composite ≥ 4452.
2-Score Composite: At least one score Proficient+, none Below Basic, composite ≥ 2939. The same Keystone score logged for Pathway 1 is used here automatically.

Pathway 3 — Career & Technical Education

CTE concentration completion with associated Keystone courses

Complete PA Cyber's courses associated with Keystone Exams (Algebra I, English 10, Biology) plus one of: Industry-Based Competency Certification, demonstration of high likelihood of success on an approved industry-based assessment, or demonstrated CTE Concentrator readiness.
Note: PA Cyber does not offer this option but will accept demonstrated completion from a previous school district with appropriate records.

Pathway 4 — Alternative Assessment

Approved alternative assessment scores plus associated Keystone courses

Complete PA Cyber's Keystone-associated courses (Algebra I, English 10, Biology) plus one of the following:
  • ACT: Score ≥ 21
  • ACT WorkKeys NCRC: Gold Level
  • ASVAB AFQT: Composite ≥ 31
  • PSAT/NMSQT: Score ≥ 970
  • SAT: Score ≥ 1010
  • AP Exam: Score ≥ 3 for each Keystone area below Proficient
  • IB Exam: Score ≥ 4 for each Keystone area below Proficient
  • Concurrent enrollment course for each Keystone area below Proficient
  • Acceptance at accredited 4-year nonprofit institution with evidence of college-level coursework ability
  • Completion of approved Pre-Apprenticeship Program

Three pieces of evidence — at least one from Section 1

Complete PA Cyber's Keystone-associated courses (Algebra I, English 10, Biology) and provide three pieces of evidence (at least one from Section 1).
