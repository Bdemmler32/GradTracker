Graduation Pathways

Pennsylvania Act 158 of 2018 — Multiple Pathways to Graduation

Pathway Eligibility: Not Yet Determined Complete at least one full pathway to meet graduation requirements.
Under Act 158 of 2018, Pennsylvania students must demonstrate readiness through at least one of four pathways. Satisfying any single pathway completely meets the graduation requirement.

All three subjects must reach Proficient or higher. Multiple sittings allowed — best qualifying score per subject is used. Benchmarks are set in Testing Benchmarks.

SAT / ACT / PSAT

Meet PA benchmark scores on at least one standardized test

SAT: 480 EBRW + 530 Math (both required). ACT: 18 English, 22 Math, 23 Reading, 23 Science (all four). PSAT 10: 430 EBRW + 480 Math. Best scores per section tracked. Edit benchmarks in Testing Benchmarks.
